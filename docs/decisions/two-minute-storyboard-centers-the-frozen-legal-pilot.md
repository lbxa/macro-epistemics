# Two-minute storyboard centers the frozen legal pilot

- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Status:** Superseded by storyboard-leads-with-human-agency-and-a-shared-future.md
- **Deciders:** Lucas
- **Area:** report, research communication

## Context

Lucas requested a Markdown storyboard for a video of at most two minutes,
with a soft melody and a 3Blue1Brown-inspired mathematical aesthetic adapted
to Apart Research. The conversation covers finance infrastructure, a completed
legal pilot and a prospective sector/model expansion. Presenting those as one
completed benchmark would blur their different evidence status.

## Decision

Prepare a 118-second storyboard centered on the frozen ContractNLI pilot's
separation of source correctness, defence revision and jury acceptance. Use
the unanimous reference-disagreement example, five-seed observations and
conditional uncertainty as the narrative; identify expansion as proposed work.

## Consequences

The [storyboard](../../report/epistemics/storyboard.md) specifies narration,
visual beats, music and evidence locators for later implementation with `anim`.
It keeps missing verdicts and conditional scope visible, uses the current
manuscript's author credits, and treats the film palette as a proposal rather
than an official brand specification. Runtime includes titles, holds and the
music tail. This change produces no animation, new experiment or model-access
claim. The paper's admitted findings are unchanged.
