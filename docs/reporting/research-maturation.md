# Preparing empirical research for peer review

Use this workflow when extending an exploratory LLM study or revisiting an
earlier project. It captures the experimental and editorial process developed
here, including the replications that narrowed the initial interpretation.
Adapt the research question, experimental unit, evidence authority and controls
to the receiving project. The procedure transfers; findings and publication
readiness must be established separately.

Here, a single financial calculation grew into a benchmark-derived,
multi-domain study with several model families and repeated seeds. Feedback
then prompted a grading audit, an integrated generator-repair rerun and a
broader judge comparison. The new comparisons narrowed the original claim
and exposed selective missingness. The paper improved by incorporating those
limitations alongside its stronger evidence.

## 1. Recover the evidence and choose the claim

Recover existing cloud jobs, journals, configurations and artifacts before
dispatching calls. Distinguish assigned, executed, failed, missing and uploaded
observations; an absent upload does not establish an unplayed run. Preserve
historical project identities and frozen cohorts.

Write one central research question in the paper brief. For each proposed
claim, record its comparison, observation unit, grading authority, eligible
denominator, uncertainty method and plausible alternative explanation. Identify
which claim needs broader evidence. A broad primary study can coexist with a
diagnostic supported by only one selected case.

## 2. Anchor tasks and validate a small experiment

Where suitable, reuse benchmark work with its documents, reference files and
grading method. Check that required assets and execution environments actually
exist; a catalog pointer is not an executable task. Audit units, rounding,
annotation scope and reference ambiguity before interpreting disagreement as
error. Use execution logs or final application state for claims about actions.
Record adaptations to benchmark grading: a scalar tutoring score does not
establish teaching quality, and a text-only task does not verify an action.

Validate the complete path on a small case: ordinary task, retained output,
intervention, response, evaluator and independent outcome check. Freeze outputs
before branching. For dispute studies, separate correctness, stated confidence,
continued support and jury acceptance. For coordination studies, separate
proposals, agreements, execution and the compromised outcome. Successful
plumbing establishes operational readiness; the scientific claim needs its
own evidence.
State whether the jury evaluates the frozen original or a revised answer;
a later correction does not retroactively repair the original claim.

Define each role's information boundary. Keep operator-only labels, references
and plans out of participant inputs unless disclosure is an explicit condition.
Audit indirect leakage through generated prompts as well as explicit fields.

## 3. Freeze an expansion that tests generalization

Before new outcomes arrive, record source selection, model/provider versions,
conditions, controls, seeds, output limits, budgets, exclusions and stopping
rules. Identify post hoc extensions honestly; do not describe an exploratory
choice as preregistered. Use new cohort identities for changed interventions
and retain the original evidence.

Expand the dimension required by the claim:

- Add independent source cases to test whether a selected example recurs.
- Add relevant benchmarks or domain strata when the claim spans those tasks.
- Add model families or crossed judges to test model dependence, recording
  capability and configuration differences.
- Add controls that distinguish competing explanations: neutral review,
  unsupported pressure, a plausible false alternative, valid counterevidence,
  or correct-claim controls, as appropriate.

Branching from the same saved answer makes comparisons interpretable. State
what matching holds fixed; benchmark matching does not also match author,
difficulty or argument style. Include a sensitivity excluding the motivating
case when it would otherwise dominate the extension.

This study used five model families, five benchmarks, four domains and five
defence seeds. Those counts describe its design, not universal requirements
for a benchmark or publication. Repeated seeds measure variation conditional
on the task; they do not create new independent tasks or eliminate randomness.
Balanced benchmark strata also do not establish representative industry or
employment-weighted performance. For longitudinal benchmarks, retain fixed
anchors alongside newly validated cases, version changes and report cohorts
separately so refreshing the test preserves interpretable comparisons.

## 4. Execute with recoverable, bounded evidence

Use the receiving project's authorized provider, cloud runner, tracking scope
and spending limits. API costs and synthetic game stakes are separate axes.
Verify the actual runner and authenticated model access: tracking alone
supplies no compute, and a catalog entry does not guarantee inference access.
Retain immutable job identities, spending reservations, live progress and
versioned artifacts so a fresh checkout can recover the campaign.

Preserve terminal failures without replacement. Retain any lossless parsing
interpretation as a hashed overlay beside the raw failure, not a replacement
generation. A larger output-budget follow-up is a separate sensitivity cohort.
Keep scripted tests and mock runs
separate from behavioral evidence, and isolate their engine stores from live
campaigns. Retain the authoritative execution evidence for claims about actual
settlements or application actions.

## 5. Analyse variation, missingness and evaluator validity

Use the actual independent unit: source group, episode, session or memory
block, according to the design. Keep dependent generations, jurors and rounds
together. Reuse the original sampling strata. Distinguish seed-level sample
SD from source-level confidence intervals and report their denominators.

Pair observations by their full experimental identity before estimating a
contrast. Equal valid counts in two conditions can come from disjoint
successful calls. Report all-assigned completion, condition-specific valid
counts and paired eligibility. Failed calls remain missing judgments; where
appropriate, show deterministic missing-outcome bounds separately from CIs.
Collapsed intervals and small strata do not establish population certainty.

Keep grading sensitivities beside literal scores. Audit whether the evaluator
encodes answers, follows a false defence, confuses reference disagreement with
actual error, or filters difficult cases through output failures. Integrate a
repair in a separately versioned affected-condition rerun. Report the remaining
oracle dependencies and collection-time confounds.

Use independent calculations or execution checks where they establish the
target. Obtain qualified human adjudication for disputed semantic judgments
that the claims depend on. Prepare source-first reviews before exposing model
responses; assess answers, explanations and citations separately. A prepared
packet or another model panel is not completed human review.

## 6. Iterate the paper alongside the experiments

For each evidence wave, repeat this cycle:

1. Collect and admit the runs with verified source and artifact hashes.
2. Inspect completion, ordinary successes, nulls, failures and selected traces.
3. Compare the observations with the claim and reviewer feedback.
4. Update the brief's claim-to-evidence map before strengthening prose.
5. Revise methods, results, captions, discussion and limitations together;
   then rewrite the abstract and contribution around the supported result.
6. Regenerate charts and tables, compile, and inspect rendered pages.

Use domain-stratified figures when they answer the question. Retain raw counts,
uncertainty and failure accounting. Put representative, source-located prompt
and response snippets beside their interpretation; quotations are selected
illustrations, not extra independent evidence or proof of internal thought.
Keep detailed ledgers and intermediate readouts in the supplement.

Expand related work around the actual design and findings, using verified
primary sources. Respond to criticism with a targeted test or a narrower
claim. Preserve a negative replication when it weakens an appealing story.
Here, additional cases limited the original dramatic judge-failure claim, and
matching exposed an incomplete comparison hidden by marginal success counts.
That correction is part of the research contribution.

## 7. Close the reproducibility and publication gaps

Bind analysis inputs to immutable artifact versions and hashes. Package the
source, dependency locks, portable observations, generated assets and exact
offline reproduction commands. Verify a fresh archive download. Exclude
credentials, recovery databases, signing material, private reasoning and
operator join keys; check source reuse terms before public release.

Track readiness separately:

| Stage | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Operational | The real execution path, recovery and accounting work |
| Experimental | Declared cohorts, controls, repeated runs and failures are retained |
| Scientific | Claims match the grading, missingness and generalization checks; required adjudication is complete |
| Submission | Authors approve the interpretation; venue format, anonymity, disclosures and release terms are satisfied |

Passing tests, adding models or building a polished PDF does not certify the
later stages. Keep remaining requirements explicit. Select a venue for the
supported contribution and follow its actual rules; an earlier sprint's page
cap and deadline do not govern a new submission.

## Applying this to an earlier project

Start with its own brief, evidence register and limitations. Make a feedback
matrix containing the disputed claim, alternative explanation, targeted
extension, grading authority, resulting artifact and revised claim. Run the
cycle above within that project's existing authorizations. Preserve its old
paper and cohorts, then produce a separately reviewed revision. Copying this
workflow does not authorize new spending or transfer this study's results.
