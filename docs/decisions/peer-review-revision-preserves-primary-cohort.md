# Peer-review revision preserves the primary cohort

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** harness, report

## Context

External feedback distinguishes the broad five-model, four-domain study from
a judge-context diagnostic on one selected arithmetic case. Lucas requested
implementing that feedback with delegated work. Disputed reference labels and
an unused objection-generator repair prevent treating all primary mismatches
as independently established errors.

## Decision

Keep the completed primary cohort and its immutable archives intact. Add
separate, versioned numerical-objection reruns and source-checked numerical
judge diagnostics, with five seeds, finite new spending ledgers and cloud
execution. Prepare blinded independent human adjudication rather than
substituting model or agent review. Reframe the paper around correctness,
confidence, correction and consensus, retaining the main domain figure.

## Consequences

The working paper can exceed the historical sprint page cap while awaiting a
final venue. Categorical checked-evidence experiments wait for adjudication;
their reference disagreements remain unresolved. New experimental results
must be admitted with source hashes before changing conclusions. TMLR is a
proposed route, not a submitted or accepted venue. Historical results are not
silently repaired, and human review cannot be marked complete autonomously.

## Implementation evidence

The completed replications narrow the original claim: GPT-OSS does not show
a comparably large defence penalty on three new error sources, while
DeepSeek's missing calls leave one paired source unresolved. Preserve both
the selected-case failure and this limited generalization. Keep deterministic
missing-outcome bounds distinct from bootstrap intervals, and do not replace
truncated calls or raise their budgets post hoc within the completed cohort.
