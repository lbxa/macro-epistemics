# Video uses on-screen guidance and relaxed instrumental music

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** report/video

## Context

After viewing the first Macro-Epistemics film, Lucas requested removal of the
AI voiceover, bottom progress indicator and footer. He asked for the on-screen
text to guide the viewer and for slower, more relaxed lo-fi music.

## Decision

Make draft two a text-led film with instrumental audio only. Remove persistent
bottom branding and progress graphics. Strengthen short explanatory statements
within the scenes and recompose the score at 54 BPM with sparse electric-piano
voicings and gentle texture. Preserve the earlier draft in the ignored `video/`
production directory.

## Consequences

The film's meaning must remain clear without narration, and its text needs time
to be read. The active export does not carry the old spoken-word subtitle track
or synthesize any new speech. Research credits remain on the closing card.
The approved light palette, frozen evidence boundaries and 118-second runtime
remain in force. Production source and assets stay inside `video/`, as recorded
in [the production decision](video-production-uses-community-manim-and-the-frozen-pilot.md).
