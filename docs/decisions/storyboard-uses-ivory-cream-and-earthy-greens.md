# Storyboard uses ivory, cream and earthy greens

- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas
- **Area:** report, video design

## Context

Lucas corrected the first storyboard's color scheme and specified a light theme:
ivory and cream backgrounds, with Apart Research's dark greens and earthy
colors. The 3Blue1Brown reference concerns the explanatory animation aesthetic;
the requested film has its own light visual identity.

## Decision

Use ivory and cream throughout the film, with forest-green text and reference
marks, moss corrections, ochre jury rings and burnt-clay error marks. Preserve
labels and shape encodings alongside color. Treat the proposed hex values as
production choices until official brand assets are selected.

## Consequences

The [storyboard](../../report/epistemics/storyboard.md) now applies the light
theme to each scene, including charts and title cards. Its 118-second timing,
narration and evidence selection remain intact. The earlier
[narrative decision](two-minute-storyboard-centers-the-frozen-legal-pilot.md)
continues to govern the research story; this record establishes the corrected
visual direction. No animation has been rendered.
