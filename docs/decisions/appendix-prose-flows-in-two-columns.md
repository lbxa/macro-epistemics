# Appendix prose flows in two columns

- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** report

## Context

The [earlier layout](two-column-paper-keeps-a-full-width-supplement.md)
gave the references a dedicated page and set all appendix material at full
width. The compact bibliography occupied only part of its page. Lucas asked
for two-column appendices and for the references to flow properly.

## Decision

Balance the references in two columns and let Appendix A begin below them on
the same page. Use two columns for appendix prose and output panels. Let
wide exhibits and multipage evidence tables span the page.

## Consequences

The [document outline](../../report/epistemics/sections/outline.tex)
starts the back matter. Column blocks end around spanning exhibits so the
multipage tables retain their repeated headers. Figure and table numbers,
captions, evidence inputs and citation keys retain their existing identities.
