\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methods}
\subsection{Source strata and models}
The cohort comprises 15 FinQA, 15 numerical TAT-QA, 30 ContractNLI,
30 SciFact and 30 MathDial source groups. Legal and science strata balance
three reference classes (ten each). SciFact's InsufficientEvidence means
no annotated support, rather than scientific falsity. MathDial uses
15 erroneous workings and 15 reference-working controls; grading covers
numerical solutions, not pedagogy.

Hash ordering precedes output inspection and excludes pilot agreements
(group identities and labels: Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}).

DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813, GLM-5.3, Kimi K2.6, GPT-OSS-120B and Inkling 2 Small
use native Cloudflare endpoints. Each receives every case once before Veracity
is mentioned. Answers, explanations, quotations and correctness probabilities
are frozen.

\subsection{Branches, forecasts and executed verdicts}
Four objections and seeds 101--105 yield
$5\times120\times4\times5=12{,}000$ assignments. Usable initials receive review
(reconsideration), pressure (evidence-free disagreement), false alternatives
(reference-wrong answers with invalid rationales) or audit (checking operations,
units, scope and exceptions, with available ContractNLI gold spans).
All use existing material and warn that disagreement alone is not evidence.

After disclosure, authors report current answers, stances, support for the
original and forecasts of its correctness and acceptance. The harness adds
a claim of source support. That wording cannot establish model-authored
overstatement.

Three GPT-OSS jurors see source, original, objection and frozen defence;
author identity, grades, peer ballots and probabilities are hidden.
Votes execute through commitment, reveal and contract settlement at fixed
1$\times$ synthetic stakes. A predeclared subset replays one frozen branch
per case with three DeepSeek jurors. Failures remain without replacement.

\subsection{Grades, endpoints and uncertainty}
\label{sec:uncertainty}
Literal grades use numerical adapters or categorical references. A post hoc
eight-case numerical sensitivity retains both grade sets
(Section~\ref{sec:grading}); neither constitutes expert adjudication.

For an original reference-match indicator $C\in\{0,1\}$, a prospective
probability $p\in[0,1]$ is scored by the Brier loss $(p-C)^2$.
Jury forecasts target acceptance instead. A concession loses an original
reference match; a correction repairs a mismatch. Declared support is separate.
Verdicts always concern the original. Missing answers and unsettled panels
retain explicit denominators.

Percentile intervals use 2,000 within-stratum source-group bootstrap resamples,
retaining seeds. Paired differences match seeds and average within source.
Sample SD summarizes seed-level aggregate rates using $n-1$, with $n\leq5$.
Collapsed intervals do not imply population certainty; provider seeds do not
establish independence.
