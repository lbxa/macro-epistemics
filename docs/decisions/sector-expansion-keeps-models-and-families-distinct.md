# Sector expansion keeps models and families distinct

- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas
- **Area:** harness, tasks, experiment design

## Context

Lucas requested deeper results grouped by sector and at least two additional
frontier models supported by Cloudflare. The pilot has three model versions
across three families. Native catalog visibility and provider-catalog visibility
do not establish inference entitlement, as the earlier Opus billing rejection
showed. Several pilot models are scheduled to retire on November 20.

## Decision

Use at least five pinned Cloudflare model versions as the expansion target and
report the number of developer families separately. Preserve independent task
grades, jury verdicts, and source-group identities when comparing occupational
domains. Keep a proposed roster distinct from models with successful inference.

## Consequences

The [sector proposal](../../harness/research/sector-expansion/README.md) recommends
Inkling 2 Small and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, yielding five versions across four
families with the existing anchors. These additions are candidates, not new
performance evidence or authorization for a larger campaign. The proposal
records source counts, grading prerequisites, retirements and request counts.
Budget and cloud-compute requirements remain in force; catalog inspection
does not authorize account funding or silently extend a local exception.

Occupational-domain comparisons are confounded with benchmark format when a
domain has only one source. Treat sector effects as exploratory until multiple
sources or matched mechanisms support the attribution. Five defence seeds do
not replace independent source documents.

## Access verification update — 2026-11-15

Lucas asked which models the supplied key could actually use. Bounded native
inference succeeded for GPT-OSS-120B, Inkling 2 Small and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash.
The [diagnostic record](../../harness/research/sector-expansion/inference-access-20261115.json)
retains receipts and failures. It used the original $10 ledger with a one-cent
diagnostic bound, keeping two failed SDK-attempt reservations. No budget was
increased and no benchmark evidence was generated. The six accessible versions
now span five developer families; the executed pilot remains a three-model
cohort. Catalog-only candidates still require their own invocation check.
