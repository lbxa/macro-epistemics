\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\textbf{Revision and persuasion answer different questions.}
Resistance coexists with low repair of reference mismatches; stability and
acceptance therefore need correctness checks. No non-Veracity control isolates
the platform's effect, and visible responses cannot identify belief or intent.

\textbf{Replication limits the persuasion claim.}
The new GPT-OSS cases weaken the initial persuasion story; selective DeepSeek
completion may conceal an effect. More calls on these few cases would not
resolve generalization. Checked evidence supplies the answer, so recovery
could reflect verification, deference, or both.

\textbf{Benchmark maintenance requires evaluator audits.}
Faulty grades can create apparent confidence failures. An offline inverse
also recovers 287 references from objections (Appendix~\ref{app:leakage}).
The repair blocks this encoding, not every leakage channel; its reference
veto remains an oracle dependency. Participant exploitation is unobserved.

\textbf{Scope and next evidence.}
Independent adjudication must resolve disputed references. Prospective
sampling, correction-only versus derivation-only controls, and varied task
formats within domains would test prevalence and recovery mechanisms.
