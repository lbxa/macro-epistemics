\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
Changing an answer can indicate correction or unjustified concession;
maintaining it can indicate justified resistance or persistence in error.
Jury acceptance resolves neither distinction.

We ask whether confidence and continued defence track reference agreement
or jury acceptance. We adapt the Veracity game~\cite{barbosa2025veracity}
to benchmark tasks. In each dispute, an author responds to an objection
and three jurors judge the original claim, even if the author corrects it.
We freeze benchmark answers before disclosing this process, then branch
them into controlled disputes.

We contribute a measurement protocol, a five-model study across 120 source
groups in four domains, and an evaluator audit. Literal grades and a post hoc
source-audit sensitivity remain separate; independent adjudication is pending.

A repaired-objection rerun and an eight-source judge replication test
evaluator failures without estimating their prevalence. The earlier ContractNLI
pilot is reported separately.
