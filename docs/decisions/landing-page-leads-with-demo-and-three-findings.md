# Landing page leads with demo and three findings

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** website

## Context

Lucas requested a further simplification after the playable Veracity demo was
added. The page should remain a concise introduction and route to the research,
rather than reproduce the manuscript's structure.

## Decision

Keep the archive header, a short abstract and the immediately following demo.
Present three compact findings, followed by the film and a compact resource
list. Put the existing interactive charts, intervals and provenance behind one
native disclosure. Keep the citation available through a separate disclosure.

## Consequences

The default reading path loses the table of contents, long methods section,
duplicated contract example, full scope panel and multiple vision subsections.
Essential denominators and limits remain beside the findings. Full statistical
detail stays available in the expandable explorer, downloadable data and paper.
The demo behavior, source snapshots and header styling remain unchanged.
