# Website summary distinguishes grading from jury acceptance

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** website, research communication

## Context

Lucas supplied a summary abstract and requested an editor pass over the website.
The public page needs to explain the primary results without treating a jury
verdict, a valid response or a source-audit grade as an infallible truth label.

## Decision

Keep the supplied abstract verbatim, with a readable left-aligned abstract block.
Clarify the surrounding chart copy: confidence is a mean stated probability;
original and post hoc audited grades remain separate; response validity concerns
format; jurors vote on the saved claim while the defence is graded separately.

## Consequences

The existing primary aggregate supplies every plotted value. The wording keeps
source-group uncertainty, outcome-specific denominators and pending independent
adjudication explicit. The leakage disclosure concerns an offline demonstration
and the unchanged primary run; it does not claim observed model exploitation.
This is a website editorial revision, not admission of new experimental results
or a change to the paper download.
