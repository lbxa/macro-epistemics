# Macro-Epistemics names the project; Veracity remains the instrument

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas
- **Area:** report, project identity

## Context

Lucas proposed macro-epistemics as a new project title because the research
covers multiple benchmarks and domains. The film also needs a clear name for
its broader human vision, while Veracity already names the dispute engine
and its existing packages and APIs.

## Decision

Use *Macro-Epistemics* as the public-facing working project and film title,
*Make AI Answerable* as its tagline, and *Veracity* for the claim-and-defence
research instrument. Explain the broader research question as how AI claims
hold up across domains and evaluators, with human benefits framed as a vision.

## Consequences

The [storyboard](../../report/epistemics/storyboard.md) distinguishes project
and instrument at the second-ten reveal and on the closing card. Its 118-second
runtime and light, earthy palette remain intact. The paper keeps its current
scientific title and evidence scope; tracking identity, repository environment,
package names and API names are unchanged. The working title does not assert
representative economy-wide coverage or a completed theory of knowledge.
