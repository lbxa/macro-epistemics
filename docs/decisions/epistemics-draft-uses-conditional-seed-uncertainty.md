## Decision

the narrowly scoped CP1--CP3 observations. Anchor the paper on Track 1's
assertion--defence gap, with jury validity as a related trust-infrastructure

defences and jury excerpts with source fields, offsets and hashes. This permits
those selected excerpts in the report snapshot; raw call logs, hidden reasoning,
credentials, complete private transcripts and spending ledgers remain excluded.
