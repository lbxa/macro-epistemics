\section{Independent reference review protocol}
\label{app:reference-adjudication}

Human adjudication is pending. The packet separates tasks/documents for all
120 sources from 581 usable original answers; 19 originals remain unavailable.

Two qualified reviewers first solve tasks independently, recording spans,
units, precision and ambiguity. Freeze both source reviews before releasing
answers. They then assess answer
correctness, explanations and citation support separately. Historical grades,
model identity, confidence, condition, seed and verdicts are hidden; source
prose may reveal its origin. Operators retain the join key and provenance.

Freeze answer reviews before reconciliation by a third qualified reviewer,
preserving initial decisions and ambiguity. Report agreement and missingness by domain; retain
benchmark and automated grades beside human labels. Blank forms, rubrics and
hashes accompany the reproducibility materials. No human judgments are
complete; the packet does not adjudicate challenge--defence trajectories.
