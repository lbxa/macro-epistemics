We test whether confidence and resistance to dispute track reference agreement
or jury acceptance. Five model families answer 120 source groups from five
benchmarks across four domains. Four challenges and five seeds yield 11,483
valid defences and 11,469 settled verdicts from 12,000 assignments.
Under a post hoc grading sensitivity, pressure loses 20/2,543 reference matches;
audit repairs 17/334 mismatches. Mean confidence exceeds reference accuracy
for four of five models in legal reasoning and all five in science. Audits change
30 initial grades and expose reference leakage through objections; a repaired
numerical rerun yields few transitions. Judge replication qualifies a selected
arithmetic failure: GPT-OSS's paired defence effect across three new errors
is $-2.2$ percentage points (95\% source-group interval $[-6.7,2.2]$).
DeepSeek's zero change covers two sources; failures leave the third unpaired.
Checked calculations help another financial case but disclose the answer.
Reference agreement, confidence, revision and acceptance require separate
measurement; disputed grades and selective missingness limit conclusions
about actual error and judge reliability.
