# Research maturation transfers the method, not the findings

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** research workflow, harness, report

## Context

Lucas wants the experimental and editorial process used here captured for
two earlier projects. The work included benchmark reuse, independent cases,
multiple model families, repeated seeds, evaluator audits, targeted reruns
and iterative report revision. Some extensions weakened the initial story;
independent human adjudication remains a distinct requirement.

## Decision

Use the [research revision workflow](../reporting/research-maturation.md)
when strengthening an exploratory study. Start from the receiving project's
question and evidence gaps, freeze separately versioned extensions, collect
and audit outcomes, and rewrite the paper around what those outcomes support.
Keep the original cohorts and reports reproducible.

## Consequences

Broader independent evidence, controls and valid grading can support stronger
claims; a larger run count alone cannot. The five-model, five-seed design here
is an example, not a universal publication threshold. Transfer the method and
its safeguards without importing conclusions, merging historical cohorts or
implying automatic peer-review readiness. Existing spend and launch authority
still applies to each project; this record does not launch new experiments.
