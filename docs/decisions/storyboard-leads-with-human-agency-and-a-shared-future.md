# Storyboard leads with human agency and a shared future

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas
- **Area:** report, research communication

## Context

Lucas requested a new goal for the two-minute film: informative and
inspirational, with a captivating first ten seconds and an avant-garde vision
of what the research could mean for humanity. A compact methods/results recap
did not give that broader ambition enough space. The light ivory/cream and
earthy-green palette remains his explicit visual direction.

## Decision

Lead with the human possibility of challenging consequential AI claims, reveal
Veracity at second ten, explain one interaction, then use admitted pilot
findings to motivate a shared future of inspectable claims and corrections.
The revised storyboard proposes the film title *Make AI Answerable* while
preserving the paper's existing title and scientific scope.

## Consequences

The [eight-scene storyboard](../../report/epistemics/storyboard.md) remains
118 seconds. Its first ten seconds receive explicit visual choreography,
and the shared-future act has 22 seconds. Pilot findings, the broader study
design and imagined human benefits are visibly distinct. Technical intervals
remain available in the paper and production notes; they no longer consume a
spoken scene. No new behavioral finding or proven social benefit is asserted.

This supersedes the earlier
[narrative emphasis](two-minute-storyboard-centers-the-frozen-legal-pilot.md).
The [light-theme decision](storyboard-uses-ivory-cream-and-earthy-greens.md)
continues to govern the visual direction. This is a storyboard revision, not
animation production or a product launch.
