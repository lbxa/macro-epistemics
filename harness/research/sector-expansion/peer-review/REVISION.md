## What the new evidence can establish

The numerical diagnostic asks whether the observed judge failure recurs on
additional frozen cases. One of its four errors is the historical trigger,
and the three new error sources are reported separately. Correct controls
distinguish improved rejection of false claims from indiscriminate rejection.
Operator-checked arithmetic is not independent expert adjudication, and this
selected cohort cannot establish prevalence in legal or scientific tasks.

The objection replication tests stability under a replacement for the
specific reference-encoding policy. Its reference veto remains an oracle
dependency. The legacy comparison is paired by source, model and seed, but
collection times differ. It does not identify a pure randomized treatment
effect or prove that models exploited the old leakage.

## Findings after admission

The trigger-excluded GPT-OSS defence change is −2.2 percentage points, with
a pointwise 95% source-group interval [−6.7, +2.2], on all 45 paired calls
across three new error sources. DeepSeek's 0.0 change uses 27/45 pairs and
only two sources. Its third source has disjoint successful calls between
contexts. All 39 failed judge calls are DeepSeek calls: 37 output limits and
two timeouts. Allowing missing judgments either value gives deterministic
bounds [−28.9, +31.1] pp for its all-assigned defence change; these are not
confidence intervals. The larger one-case effect cannot be presented as a
general effect across these sources.

The repaired numerical condition changes paired pooled audited loss from
3/1,333 to 1/1,333 and repair from 3/32 to 4/32. These sparse changes preserve
the descriptive pattern without establishing policy equivalence or actual
error rates. All four selected correct controls receive 60/60 correct
judgments per model/context. Checked evidence improves the GS financial case
but does not universally repair judgments. Full case-level counts, including
failures, remain in the paper and portable snapshot.

## Remaining human and publication steps

Qualified people must complete and reconcile the blinded source and response
reviews before actual-error claims replace reference-relative findings.
Complete trajectory adjudication remains outside that packet. Authors must
review the scientific interpretation and confirm venue, prior-publication
status, author metadata, conflicts, licensing and the final release. No
submission, publication approval or external reviewer recruitment has been
performed by this revision.
