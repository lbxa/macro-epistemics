# Macro-Epistemics title describes confidence under dispute

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas
- **Area:** report, project identity

## Context

Lucas requested an avant-garde, objective and concise report title, suggested
including macro-epistemics, and delegated the final wording. The active study
covers five model families and four occupational domains; it does not establish
representative industry performance or a theory of economy-wide epistemics.

## Decision

Use **Macro-Epistemics: Confidence Under Dispute** as the scientific report
title, consistently in the visible heading, LaTeX title and PDF metadata.
The subtitle names the research question without implying a completed dispute
phase, a calibration result or a general model ranking.

## Consequences

This replaces only the paper-title choice in the earlier
[project identity record](macro-epistemics-names-the-project-veracity-remains-the-instrument.md).
Macro-Epistemics remains the project identity and Veracity the instrument.
The film's *Make AI Answerable* tagline and all scientific evidence boundaries
remain separate from the report title. Historical review records retain the
previous title for provenance.
