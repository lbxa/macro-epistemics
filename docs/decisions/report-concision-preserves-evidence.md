# Concise prose preserves the complete evidence

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** report

## Context

Lucas requested a high-signal paper with shorter paragraphs, without arbitrary
word cutting. The expanded draft repeated results and qualifications across
the introduction, results, discussion and conclusion.

## Decision

Give each section a distinct job and retain the shortest complete argument.
Keep essential qualifications beside their findings and detailed evidence
in the appendix, preserving all data tables, figures and frozen model excerpts.

## Consequences

The discussion interprets results rather than repeating their counts. Word
reduction must not hide missingness, grading uncertainty or replication limits,
or rely on smaller fonts. Editorial revisions leave admitted evidence and
archived manuscripts unchanged. See [writing guidance](../reporting/writing.md)
and the [active review record](../../report/epistemics/review.md).
